1 · Contrast — the biggest legibility defect

--text-muted carries almost every label on the site, and most of those labels are 10px uppercase with wide tracking — the worst possible case. On the near-black background it sits far below the WCAG AA floor of 4.5:1 for small text. Raising it one step fixes the most-read text on the page.

2 · The 9–12px label cluster

Five ad-hoc sizes (9 / 10 / 11 / 12px) are used interchangeably for the same jobs — labels, badges, metadata. The result is that no two screens agree on what a “small label” is. Collapse to a two-token scale: label = 11px, metadata = 12px.

9px · text-[9px] Terminal status dot — too small
10px · text-[10px] All section labels, badges, telemetry keys
11px · text-[11px] Meta lines — same job as the 10px above
12px · text-xs Chips & status — same job again
proposed metadata 12px label 11px
Keep the wide tracking you already have; on light-on-dark add one weight step at the smallest size to compensate for optical thinning.

3 · Reading measure — the course/lesson surface

This is the only true Read content on the site, and it currently runs prose at 12–14px across a max-w-4xl container. That means a measure well past 100 characters. Comfortable reading is 45–75ch, and 16px is the practical floor.

4 · Font roster

RoleNow (A)Recommended (B)Why
Display / headings Space Grotesk Chakra Petch Notched and technical — futuristic without kitsch; strong in large uppercase; off the generic shortlist.
Body / UI Inter IBM Plex Sans Clearly stronger at 12–14px; true tabular figures for telemetry; humanist-industrial, not neutral-generic.
Mono / labels · data · nav JetBrains Mono IBM Plex Mono Same superfamily as the body → one coherent voice; crisp at 10–12px caps.
Serif (used once) Playfair Display IBM Plex Serif / or drop Playfair appears exactly once and reads as a foreign object; Plex Serif italic keeps a pull-quote voice inside the family.
Net: 3 coherent families instead of 4 half-used ones, distinctly “security engineer” rather than default-template, and a measurable legibility win at the sizes the UI actually uses.
